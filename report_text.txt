2.2 Application Architecture
The application follows a role-based architecture with three distinct portals:

Doctor Portal (9 pages) — /doctor/*
- 11-tab EHR system with comprehensive clinical data entry (infertility history, semen analysis, scans, OPU, embryology, outcomes, billing)
- IVF cycle monitoring with hormone trends and embryo development tracking
- Nurse task coordination board
- Lab reports review and approval workflow
- Critical care dashboard, cryo inventory, AI embryo selection

Nurse Portal (7 pages) — /nurse/*
- 4-step patient onboarding wizard with KYC document upload, NARTSR enrollment, marriage certificate verification
- Task queue with doctor-assigned duties
- Medication adherence tracking and confirmation
- Document verification queue
- Patient complaints console

Patient Portal (5 pages) — /patient/*
- Personal cycle progress dashboard
- Medication schedule with adherence tracking
- Lab reports viewer
- Appointment calendar
- Complaint submission system

Authentication routes users to their appropriate portal on login. The database contains 30 tables (26 core clinical + 4 audit/security) managed via Supabase PostgreSQL.
